Good To Know

Laundry & Dry Cleaning FAQ

Do you do pickup and drop-off?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp with your address and we'll arrange pickup and drop-off in Cyberjaya, Putrajaya, Bangi, Seri Kembangan, Dengkil, Gamuda Cove and Serenia City.

How long does dry cleaning take?

Most orders are ready within 24–48 hours. For urgent items, ask us on WhatsApp about express dry cleaning.

Do you clean comforters and carpets?

Yes. We clean comforters, toto and bulky bedding, plus household and office carpets. Send us a photo for the best option.

Do you do alterations and stitching?

Yes. We do alterations and fitting for existing garments, and custom stitching for new clothes.

How do I get a quote?

Send a photo of your item on WhatsApp and we'll advise the best service and give you a quick quote.

Not sure which service you need?

Send us a photo on WhatsApp and we'll advise the best option.
