Good To Know

Alterations FAQ

How much do alterations cost?

Hemming trousers or jeans starts at RM10, replacing a zip is from RM10 and adding a button is from RM5. Jacket sleeve or length adjustments are from RM60. Prices are listed above, or send a photo for a quote.

Do you do both alterations and new stitching?

Yes. We adjust existing clothes and stitch new clothes made to fit.

How long do alterations take?

Simple alterations can often be done quickly. Ask us on WhatsApp and we will confirm the timing.

How do I get a quote?

Send a photo of the garment and what you want changed on WhatsApp and we will get back to you.
