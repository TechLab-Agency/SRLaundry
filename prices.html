Good To Know

Price FAQ

How much does wash and fold cost in Cyberjaya?

Normal wash and fold is RM3.50 per kg. Towels and bedsheets are RM6.00 per kg, and curtains and sofa covers are RM8.00 per kg.

How much does ironing cost in Cyberjaya?

Ironing with a hanger and ironing fold is RM2.40 per piece, and ironing without a hanger is from RM1.80 per piece. Jubbah, long dress and coat are RM5 to RM10, and bedsheets and duvet covers are from RM8.

How much does dry cleaning cost in Cyberjaya?

Dry cleaning starts from RM5 for a scarf and from RM7.50 for a shirt, pants or T-shirt. A baju Melayu set is RM14, a baju kurung set is from RM15, a coat is RM14 and an overcoat is from RM30.

How much do alterations cost?

Hemming trousers or jeans starts at RM10, replacing a zip is from RM10 and adding a button is from RM5. Jacket sleeve or length adjustments are from RM60. Send a photo for a quote.

Are these the final prices?

Prices marked “from” or “+” start at that amount. The final price depends on the item and its condition. Send us a photo on WhatsApp and we will confirm.
